# Caimack brand policy

The name **Caimack**, the vinyl-C logo and the handwritten Caimack wordmark are
used to identify the official Caimack project maintained by Bulat Ruslanovich.
This policy does not assert that any mark is registered, or that exclusive rights
exist in every jurisdiction.

This is a brand policy, not an amendment to the AGPL. It does not restrict rights
granted by the software license, override prior licenses, or limit descriptive,
nominative or other uses permitted by applicable law.

## Uses that do not require separate permission under this policy

- Referring truthfully to Caimack in articles, reviews, tutorials, presentations
  and documentation. Linking to the official project is welcome.
- Showing accurate product screenshots, without suggesting an endorsement or
  claiming rights to third-party artwork shown inside the application.
- Running an unmodified Caimack instance for yourself, your family or your
  organization with its existing in-application branding. This does not make
  the instance an official service.
- Describing a fork or integration as "based on Caimack", "a fork of Caimack"
  or "compatible with Caimack", when accurate and presented as a reference
  rather than the product's own brand.
- Preserving copyright, license and attribution notices required by law or the
  applicable software license.

## Public forks, modified distributions and services

Use your own primary product name and logo for a publicly promoted modified
distribution or third-party commercial service. You may acknowledge Caimack in
the description. Make clear who operates the service and that it is independent
from the official Caimack project.

For example: **"Example Music — an independent service based on Caimack."**

Separate written permission is needed under this policy to use the Caimack name
as the primary brand of such a product, or its logo/wordmark as that product's
identity. These permissions are distinct from permission to use the code.

## Do not misrepresent the relationship

- Do not claim a fork, service, build or app-store listing is the official
  Caimack project, certified by it or endorsed by its maintainer without consent.
- Do not use the logo as your own business or product identity without permission.
- Do not use a confusing domain, account name, app listing or presentation that
  impersonates the official project. An accurate explanatory reference is fine.

## Asking for permission

Contact the maintainer through the official repository:
<https://github.com/BulatRuslanovich/music-streaming>.
For requests involving private or commercial details, arrange a private contact
with the maintainer rather than posting those details publicly.

Trademark availability, ownership, enforcement and any registration are separate
legal questions governed by the relevant jurisdiction. This policy does not
create a registration, guarantee protection or revoke rights already granted.
